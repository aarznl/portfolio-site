In Stanford's ME218A: Smart Product Design Fundamentals, a course focused on mechatronics, I built an electromechanical arcade game where users are tasked with cooking three pans without burning them.

The full project report for **Calcifer's Cook-Off**, including game run-throughs, is on the [project site](https://sites.google.com/stanford.edu/25me218aprojectteam7).
