In Stanford's ME218B: Smart Product Design Applications, a course focused on mechatronics, I built an autonomous robot that detects beacons, follows lines, collects balls, and dispenses them into buckets on a game field.

The full project report for **Purple Tape**, including game run-throughs, is on the [project site](https://sites.google.com/stanford.edu/me218bpurpletape/overview).
