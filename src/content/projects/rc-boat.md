In Stanford's ME218C: Smart Product Design Practice, a course focused on mechatronics, I built a remote-controlled boat that collects floating rubber toys in a pool of water, driven by a remote controller that communicates with it over UART.

The full project report for **Mr. QuackWide**, including game run-throughs, is on the [project site](https://sites.google.com/stanford.edu/me-218c-team-3/home).
